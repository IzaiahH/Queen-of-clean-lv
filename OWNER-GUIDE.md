# Owner's guide

A short guide to running Queen of Clean LV day to day. Sign in at **yourdomain.com/admin**.

## Before you go live (one time)

1. **Settings → Business.**
   - Check your business name, phone, email, hours and "About" text.
   - Only list facts you can stand behind. Leave out licenses, insurance or awards unless you have them.
   - Upload your logo if you prefer it to the built-in crown.
2. **Pricing & Services.**
   - The starting prices (weekly $165, every 2 weeks $185, monthly $210, Queen Reset $275) are a draft for you to review.
   - Click **Start editing prices** and adjust the draft.
   - Try a few homes in the **Test** panel to compare against live prices, then click **Publish**.
3. **Settings → Policies.** Read the cancellation, rescheduling, terms and privacy text, edit it, then tick **reviewed**. Until you do, the public pages say the policies are being finalized.
4. **Settings → Booking.**
   - Set your working days and hours, notice period and daily job limit.
   - Choose whether online requests need your review (the default) or confirm automatically.
5. **Cleaners.**
   - Add each cleaner with their working hours, time off, the services they do and how they're paid.
   - Worker type is up to you and your accountant; the app doesn't assume contractor or employee status.
6. **Settings → Team logins.** Give a cleaner a login so they can see only their own jobs on their phone. Office helpers can get an "Office" login, which has no access to money, pricing or settings.
7. **Import / Export.** If you keep customers in a spreadsheet:
   - Upload it (CSV or Excel).
   - Match the columns and review every flagged row, then import.
   - Imported customers are marked "Imported" with the spreadsheet values in their private notes. Nothing is scheduled until you book it.
8. **Settings → Emails.** Use **Send me a test** to make sure email is working. Every email shows as Sent, Not sent or Failed, and nothing claims to be sent when it wasn't.

## Every day

- **Dashboard.** Shows today's jobs, requests waiting for review, anything that needs attention, and this month's numbers.
- **New online requests** arrive as **Pending review**. Open one from the dashboard or calendar, then confirm it (the customer gets an email) or decline it with a message.
- **Calendar.**
  - Switch between Month, Week, Day and Agenda (phones open on Agenda).
  - Drag a job to move it. You'll be asked to confirm and whether to tell the customer.
  - If a move causes a conflict, you'll see why before deciding.
  - Use **Block time** for days off or appointments.
- **Assign a cleaner** from the job panel. The app won't let one cleaner be in two places at once.
- **After a job:** open it, click **Mark complete** and enter the amount charged, time spent and supplies, and tick the checklist. This is what fills in your profit numbers. Record payments on the job or the customer's page.

## Recurring customers

- From any confirmed job, choose **Make recurring**. You can also set **Repeats** when creating an appointment.
  - Frequency can be weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly, quarterly or every N days.
- **Changing visits:**
  - Change **one visit** by moving or editing it. The rest stay the same.
  - Use **Edit schedule** to change "this and future" or "all upcoming" visits.
  - **Skip** a single visit, **Pause** the whole schedule (resume later), or **Cancel** it.
- Visits are created about 8 weeks ahead and topped up automatically each morning.

## Quotes

- Customers can get an instant estimate online and save it.
- You can also make one in **Quotes → New quote**.
- **Adjust price** requires a reason, and the change is recorded with your name.
- **Send** emails the customer a private link where they can accept or decline. When they accept, use **Book this quote** to put it on the calendar.

## Money

- **Revenue & Expenses**:
  - **Earned** is what completed jobs brought in.
  - **Booked** is what's on the calendar.
  - **Profit** subtracts cleaner pay, supplies and other costs.
  - Add general expenses (gas, supplies, insurance) here too.
  - The **Unpaid** list shows completed jobs still waiting for payment.
- **Reports** break things down by month, service, customer, cleaner and area. Each can be downloaded as a spreadsheet (CSV).

## Good to know

- Customer entry instructions and private notes are only visible to your team. They're never in emails, on the public site or in customer exports.
- Each email to a customer has a fresh "manage my booking" link. Older links stop working, which keeps forwarded emails safe.
- Customers can use their link to ask for a new time. Reschedule requests show on your dashboard, and nothing moves until you approve.
- Customers can also cancel from their link. Cancellations made with enough notice (your cancellation-notice setting) take effect right away. Later ones come to you as a request.
- Text messages and online card payments aren't connected yet. Record payments by hand.
